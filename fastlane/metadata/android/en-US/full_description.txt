Activity Trace lets you search across everything that happens on your phone — notifications, screenshots, clipboard copies, and downloaded files — using natural language. No AI, no cloud, no account. Everything stays encrypted on-device with SQLCipher + Android Keystore.

Features:
• Search notifications from any app
• Search clipboard history
• Search screenshot text (OCR)
• Search downloaded documents and code files
• Natural language date parsing ("yesterday", "last week")
• Full encryption at rest
• No internet permission — zero data leaves your device
• Open source (GPL-3.0)
